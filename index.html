Features

Focused tools for inventory work

The Design Partner v1 is deliberately limited to inventory, stock operations, and assisted import.

Products & categories

Maintain products, base units, photos, and tenant-specific categories.

Locations & areas

Model storage sites with nested areas up to eight levels. Stock belongs only in leaf areas.

Lot-level balances

See the exact base-unit quantity for one stock lot in one leaf area, including expiry.

Stock operations

Record adjustments, transfers, and compensating corrections through one immutable history.

Tenant administration

Manage invited members, roles, and settings within clear tenant boundaries.

Assisted Smart Import

Prepare product and stock proposals for human review. Established stock is never overwritten automatically.

The flow

From source data to explainable stock

Every inventory change is validated and follows the same operation rules.

  1. 01

    Structure

    Define products, base units, stock lots, locations, and the leaf areas that can hold stock.

  2. 02

    Review

    Inspect assisted-import proposals and resolve uncertain product, lot, and area matches before approval.

  3. 03

    Record

    Submit an adjustment, transfer, or compensating correction. Invalid or negative results are rejected.

  4. 04

    Explain

    Use immutable operation history to understand the exact balance held in each leaf area.

The difference

Focused on trustworthy inventory

Stocket v1 favors exact quantities, explicit stock operations, and a history that remains explainable.

Exact by design

Base-unit quantities retain their decimal value, are never silently rounded, and can never fall below zero.

One path for stock changes

Manual work and approved imports follow the same operation rules and update balances atomically.

Deliberately focused

The release stays online and inventory-only. Broader business workflows are documented as out of scope.
